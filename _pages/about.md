Suyash Pradhan is a third year Ph.D student in Computer Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin. He works as a Research Assistant under the guidance of Professor Kaushik Chowdhury in GENESYS Lab. My research revolves around exploring the intersection between Artifical Intelligence (AI) and Next Generation (5G / 6G) Wireless Communication Systems. 

Research Focus
-======

- Deployment of AI on real-world wireless experimental testbeds - Validating ML solutions on practical cellular networks, leveraging software-defined radios
- Over-the-Air Federated Learning - Spectrum-efficient collaborative learning by aggregating ML models trained on local devices directly during signal propagation, without a central server
- Digital Twins for V2X networks : Emulation of autonomous car environment to generate synthetic image and LiDAR samples, Ray Tracing to model propagation effects, Sim2Real Domain Adaptation
- AI-RAN - Integrating deep learning with 5G-based communication systems, including O-RAN, for adaptive end-to-end communication
